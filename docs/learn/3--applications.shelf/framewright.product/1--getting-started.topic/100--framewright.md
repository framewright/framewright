---
title: Framewright
type: concept
description: What Framewright Studio is for, and how its projects are organized.
---

Framewright Studio is a photo and video editor for montages: long sequences
of photos and video clips cut to music. It brings photo editing, video
editing and compositing into one application, with light 3D for titles and
effects.

A project holds a media library and any number of documents. Each document
has a kind, such as video or photo, and the kind decides which workspaces it
offers. To work on one kind of content inside another, you step into a
nested container instead of switching applications. For example, a video
clip opens into its own node graph on the Compose page.

Framewright is early in development. This documentation grows as features
are built.
